About This Study

This survey is part of a research project conducted by BS Criminology students at the University of Lahore. The study aims to explore the relationship between drug abuse and criminal behaviour among youth in Lahore.

Objectives

  • To assess the awareness of drug abuse effects among youth.
  • To evaluate the correlation between drug abuse and criminal activities.
  • To identify the demographic factors associated with drug abuse.
  • To recommend preventive measures and interventions.

Methodology

The study will use a mixed-methods approach, including quantitative surveys and qualitative interviews, to collect data from a diverse sample of participants aged 18-29 in Lahore.

Participants will be recruited through online platforms, social media, and community outreach. The survey will be anonymous, and all responses will be kept confidential.

Data collection is expected to be completed by December 2023, followed by data analysis and report writing.

Expected Outcomes

The study aims to provide insights into the patterns and predictors of drug abuse and criminal behaviour among youth in Lahore. It is expected to identify key risk and protective factors, as well as gaps in current prevention and intervention efforts.

The findings will be disseminated through academic publications, community reports, and presentations to stakeholders, including policymakers, educators, and healthcare providers.
